About Primetime Trading

Built around watches.
Focused on value.

Primetime Trading is a Dubai-based trading business focused on branded watches and timepieces. Our approach is to bring together recognized brands, attractive designs and competitive value for customers.

We aim to develop long-term relationships with customers, retailers and business partners through dependable products, professional service and a carefully selected brand portfolio.
